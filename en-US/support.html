Support

Check the current device, permission and route state before changing a rule.

  1. Check the chosen target and actual device separately. A saved rule does not mean that audio is routed.
  2. For app input, select that app’s AudioLock input in its own audio settings. A device name is not an access-control boundary.
  3. If a target is unavailable, reconnect it or choose another target explicitly. Do not assume another app has stopped recording.
  4. If requesting help, include app/build, macOS version, device names, direction and steps. Do not include audio recordings, account details or purchase credentials.

Open support

GitHub Issues and attachments are public. Do not post audio recordings, Apple Account or payment details, credentials, or other private information.
