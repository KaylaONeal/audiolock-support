Privacy

Audio stays on your Mac. AudioLock routes audio in memory; it does not save audio recordings or upload audio to the developer.

Local settings

Device identifiers, app rules and preferences are saved locally. Access and trial evidence are kept locally or obtained from verified StoreKit transactions.

Audio and diagnostics

Routing buffers contain temporary audio samples in memory. AudioLock does not create recording files, send audio to developer servers or include developer analytics. Diagnostics describe device and route state; do not submit private audio with a report.

Apple purchases

Apple handles StoreKit transactions and Apple Account/payment information under Apple’s policies. This is separate from local audio routing.

Control

You can pause routing or quit. Removing a selected input alias may cause the target app to choose its own fallback; check that app’s input selection. Clearing app settings does not cancel a purchase or erase Apple’s transaction history.

Privacy text for review. The final signed build and all network/diagnostic paths must be audited before production publication.
