How it is intended to work

Open AudioLock from the menu bar to see your global devices and app rules together. Each row has an input and an output. Choose devices in place, or add an app from the bottom of the panel.

Keep your preferred defaults Choose a microphone and an output in the Global row to pin them independently. If macOS changes a pinned default, AudioLock restores your choice while the device is available. Choose “Don’t pin” whenever you want that direction to switch freely.

Give supported apps their own devices Route one app to headphones and another to speakers. Choose a separate microphone for each supported app. For input, first select the corresponding “AudioLock · App” device in that app’s own audio settings. After this one-time setup, change its source in AudioLock. Apps without selectable input devices are not supported by this input method.

See what needs attention • Keep input and output choices together in each app’s row. • See waiting, setup and active states beside the device. • Keep saved choices when a device disconnects. • Wait muted when an app output target is unavailable, rather than intentionally switching it to another output. • Open routing details when you need help, or pause routing from the menu.

Global pins manage macOS defaults; apps that explicitly choose another device may ignore them. Protected or otherwise uncapturable output may not be routable. Compatibility and added latency depend on the app and device. AudioLock cannot change every third-party app’s internal microphone selection or guarantee third-party apps’ fallback behavior.

Common questions

Will every app follow a locked default?

No. Apps that explicitly choose a device can ignore the system default. Independent input requires selecting the supplied AudioLock input in a supported app.

Can two apps use different inputs and outputs at once?

That is the full-version goal. It remains unverified until two real apps pass simultaneous four-path testing; a saved rule is not proof of delivery.

Will disconnected headphones send sound to the speakers?

The output design keeps capture active and discards audio while waiting for the target. Real disconnect and crash behavior still requires verification; there is no blanket no-speaker guarantee.

Is there a free trial?

No new free trial is offered. Previously claimed trials remain valid for their existing access period; they do not enroll you in a paid subscription.

Do paid subscriptions renew automatically?

Yes. Monthly and yearly subscriptions renew automatically until canceled. Manage or cancel them in your Apple Account settings. Both plans unlock the same functions. No new trial is offered.

What happens when access expires?

Existing audio routes and device locks continue. After expiry, new routes or device locks require a purchase. Restore Purchases is available for your purchases.
